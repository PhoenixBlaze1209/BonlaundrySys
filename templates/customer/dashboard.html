Equipment Protection & Customer Liability Notice

Station Policy

Bon Laundry Station operates high-capacity commercial washers and dryers. To preserve machine integrity and avoid costly mechanical jamming, all patrons and drop-off customers must strictly follow the equipment rules below:

1. Empty All Pockets

Check and empty pockets of coins, keys, hairpins, clips, screws, and hard plastic or metal items before washing.

2. Customer Liability

If a machine is broken, dented, or jammed due to foreign objects or improper overloading, the customer who caused the damage is solely liable for repair costs.

3. Staff Non-Liability

Station attendants process laundry according to standard care; staff and station management are not liable for machine damage resulting from customer negligence.
